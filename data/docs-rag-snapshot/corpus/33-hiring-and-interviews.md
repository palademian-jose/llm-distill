# Hiring and the Interview Loop

Owner: Engineering Leadership and Talent

## Process overview

The engineering interview loop has four stages and is designed to complete
within three weeks of the first conversation:

1. **Recruiter screen** (30 minutes): role, compensation range, logistics.
2. **Technical screen** (60 minutes): a practical coding exercise in the
   candidate's preferred language, conducted in a shared editor.
3. **Virtual onsite** (half a day): four interviews, described below.
4. **Decision**: the hiring committee meets within two business days of the
   onsite.

## The onsite interviews

- **Coding** (60 minutes): extend a small, realistic codebase. No puzzles,
  no whiteboard algorithms.
- **System design** (60 minutes): design a service for a stated product
  need; senior candidates are expected to discuss failure modes and cost.
- **Collaboration** (45 minutes): walk through a past project with an
  engineer and a product manager.
- **Values** (45 minutes): behavioural questions with a hiring manager from a
  different team.

Candidates may use documentation and search during coding interviews. The
interview rubric is shared with the candidate before the onsite.

## Interviewers

Interviewers complete interviewer training (two hours) and shadow two
interviews before leading one. Each panel includes at least one interviewer
from outside the hiring team. Interviewers submit written feedback within 24
hours and before reading anyone else's feedback.

## Hiring committee

The committee is three senior engineers plus the hiring manager. It reads all
feedback and makes a hire or no-hire decision by majority; the hiring manager
does not have a veto. Level is decided separately by the committee using the
career ladder, not by the hiring manager.

## Take-home exercises

Larkspur does not use take-home exercises. Candidates who prefer to show
existing work may submit a public project in place of the technical screen.

## Referrals

Employees can refer candidates through the `referrals` form. A referral
bonus is paid after the new hire completes 90 days. Referrers do not
interview their referrals and are not told the outcome before the candidate.

## Candidate experience

Every candidate who completes an onsite receives a decision and, on request,
a summary of the feedback. Reimbursement for any expenses is processed within
ten business days.
