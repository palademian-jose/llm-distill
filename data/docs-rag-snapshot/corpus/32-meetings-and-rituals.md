# Meetings and Engineering Rituals

Owner: Engineering Leadership

## Principles

Meetings are expensive. Every recurring meeting has an owner, an agenda
published at least a day in advance and a written outcome. Any attendee may
leave a meeting that has no agenda. Decisions made in meetings are written
down in the relevant channel or document; a decision that is not written
down did not happen.

## Team rituals

- **Daily sync** (15 minutes, optional): asynchronous by default in the
  team channel; synchronous only if the team chooses. Format: what moved,
  what is blocked.
- **Planning** (weekly, 45 minutes): the team selects work for the week from
  the backlog. Tickets are estimated in T-shirt sizes (S, M, L); L tickets are
  split before being started.
- **Retrospective** (every two weeks, 45 minutes): what to start, stop and
  continue. At least one action item per retro, reviewed at the next one.

## Organisation rituals

- **Engineering Forum** (every other Thursday, 60 minutes): demos and short
  talks open to everyone in the company. Slots are ten minutes; sign up in
  `#eng-forum`.
- **Operations review** (weekly, 30 minutes): SRE and team leads review
  incidents, paging volume and SLO status.
- **Architecture Review Board** (weekly, Thursdays): RFC decisions.
- **All-hands** (monthly): company update, with an engineering segment.

## Focus time

Wednesdays are meeting-free across engineering, except for incidents. Teams
are encouraged to keep at least two further half-days per week free of
recurring meetings.

## Working hours and time zones

Engineering spans Europe and North America. Core collaboration hours are
14:00 to 17:00 UTC, when every team is expected to be reachable. Meetings
outside core hours require the agreement of everyone invited; recurring
meetings outside core hours are not allowed.

## Asynchronous communication

Slack is for conversation, not for decisions or documentation. Anything that
needs to outlive the week goes in a document, a ticket or the handbook.
Expected response time in Slack is the same business day; nobody is expected
to respond outside their working hours, and `@channel` is reserved for
incidents.

## Meeting hygiene

Cameras are optional. Meetings start on time and end five minutes early.
Notes are taken in a shared document linked from the invite, and the owner
posts the outcome within one hour of the meeting ending.
