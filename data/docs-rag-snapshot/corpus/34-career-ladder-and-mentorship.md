# Career Ladder and Mentorship

Owner: Engineering Leadership

## Levels

The engineering ladder has six individual-contributor levels and a parallel
management track starting at level 4:

| Level | Title | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | Engineer I | tasks within a feature, with guidance |
| L2 | Engineer II | features within a service, independently |
| L3 | Senior Engineer | a service or large feature; mentors L1-L2 |
| L4 | Staff Engineer | a product area; leads RFCs across teams |
| L5 | Senior Staff Engineer | multiple areas; sets technical direction |
| L6 | Principal Engineer | company-wide; sits on the Architecture Review Board |

Each level is described along four dimensions: technical skill, impact,
collaboration and leadership. The full rubric is in the `careers`
repository.

## Promotions

Promotions happen in two cycles per year, in March and September. The
engineer and their manager assemble a packet with examples against the next
level's rubric; a calibration group of managers and senior engineers from
other teams reviews packets for consistency. Promotion to L4 and above
requires written support from two engineers outside the candidate's team.

## Performance reviews

Reviews are written twice a year and are separate from promotion packets.
Each review includes self-assessment, manager assessment and peer feedback
from three to five colleagues the engineer chooses. Ratings use a three-point
scale: needs improvement, meets expectations, exceeds expectations.

## Mentorship programme

Every engineer at L1 or L2 is paired with a mentor at L3 or above from a
different team for a six-month cycle. Mentors and mentees meet for 30 minutes
every two weeks. The programme is opt-in for mentors and run by the
Engineering Leadership team; pairings are announced in January and July.

## Growth budget

Each engineer has an annual learning budget of 1,500 USD for books, courses
and conferences, plus up to five working days for conference attendance.
Requests over 500 USD need manager approval; everything else is
self-approved and expensed.

## Internal mobility

Engineers may move teams after 12 months in their current team. The engineer
talks to the receiving manager first, then to their current manager; the
transfer happens at the next quarter boundary unless both managers agree to
an earlier date. Level and compensation are unchanged by a transfer.
