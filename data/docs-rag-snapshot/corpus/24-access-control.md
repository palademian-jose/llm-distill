# Access Control

Owner: Security

## Principles

Access follows least privilege and is granted to groups, not individuals.
Every grant has an owner, a justification and an expiry. Access that has not
been used for 60 days is revoked automatically by the `access-reaper` job and
the owner is notified.

## Identity

All systems authenticate through the company identity provider with single
sign-on and hardware security keys. Passwords alone are never sufficient.
Service accounts are created through the `service-accounts` Terraform module
and are owned by a team, never by a person.

## Groups

Groups are defined in the `access` repository as code. The standard groups
for a team named `payments` are:

- `payments-eng` - all engineers on the team; read access to the team's
  `dev` and `staging` resources.
- `payments-oncall` - current primary and secondary; production read access
  and the ability to run documented runbook actions.
- `payments-admin` - the team lead and one deputy; production write access
  through break-glass only.

## Requesting access

Open a request in the `access-requests` form with the group, the reason and
the duration (maximum 90 days for temporary access, or "standing" for
role-based access). Requests are approved by the group owner and, for any
production group, also by Security. Approval is typically within one business
day.

## Break-glass

Break-glass access grants temporary elevated permissions for an incident or a
documented operational task. It is requested with `lark access break-glass
<group> --ticket <id> --for 2h`, approved by the Security on-call, and expires
automatically. Every command run under break-glass is recorded in the audit
log and reviewed the next business day.

## Quarterly review

Group owners review their membership every quarter. The review is a pull
request against the `access` repository generated by the `access-review`
bot; unreviewed groups lose production permissions after 14 days.

## Leavers

When an employee leaves, the identity provider disables their account at the
end of their last day, which revokes all SSO access immediately. The
`offboarding` checklist also covers API keys they created, which are rotated,
and repositories they solely owned, which are reassigned.
